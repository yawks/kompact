<p><b>Kompact</b> is a powerful, privacy-first offline image editor and file compressor.</p>

<p>Designed to run entirely on your device, your files never leave your phone, ensuring complete privacy. It goes far beyond simple compression, offering a comprehensive suite of photo editing and optimization tools.</p>

<p><b>✨ Key Features:</b></p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Precision Compression:</b> Target specific file sizes or quality levels to drastically save storage without noticeable quality loss.</li>
  <li><b>Smart Image Editing:</b> Crop, rotate, flip, and resize images with pixel-perfect accuracy.</li>
  <li><b>Color Filters & Adjustments:</b> Apply visual filters to enhance your photos directly within the app.</li>
  <li><b>Text Overlays:</b> Add custom text/watermarks to your images quickly.</li>
  <li><b>EXIF Metadata Management:</b> View, preserve, or securely strip away sensitive EXIF metadata (like GPS location and camera details) before sharing.</li>
  <li><b>Format Conversion:</b> Seamlessly convert your pictures between formats (JPEG, PNG, WEBP, etc.).</li>
  <li><b>Material You Design:</b> Beautiful, modern, and intuitive user interface supporting both light and dark themes.</li>
</ul>

<p>Whether you need to shrink a huge photo to send via email, remove location tags, or edit an image, Kompact handles it securely offline.</p>
