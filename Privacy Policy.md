This Privacy Policy applies to the Kompact app (hereby referred to as "Application") created by Naxod Lab (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Open Source app. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to function completely offline.

The Service Provider does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information or usage data. No data is sent to external servers or third parties. All image processing (compression and conversion) is performed locally on your device.

Permissions and Device Access

To function primarily as an image compressor and converter, the Application requires access to your device's storage (specifically images/photos).

Storage/Media Access: This permission is strictly used to select the images you wish to compress/convert and to save the resulting files back to your device.

The Application does not access any other files or data on your device unrelated to the specific images you select for processing.

Third-Party Services

The Application does not use any third-party services that collect data (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or advertising networks).

Security

Since the Application works offline and does not transmit data over the internet, your original images and processed files remain exclusively on your device. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure and the security of the device itself is the user's responsibility.

Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. Since the Application does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

Open Source Transparency

Kompact is an Open Source project. The source code is available for audit to verify that no data collection mechanisms exist.
